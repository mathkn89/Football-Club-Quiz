Privacy Policy

Football Club Quiz for Android · Last updated 4 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts and sends none of your quiz data anywhere. Your name, history, scores and settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads may be personalised. If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment.

Who is responsible

Football Club Quiz is made by an independent developer, mathkn, who is the data controller for the app. For any question about privacy or this policy, email mathias@riebe.no.

Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device. It is never uploaded to us, and we cannot see it:

  • The display name you type on the History screen
  • Your quiz history: rounds played, scores, the questions asked and the answers you gave
  • Your daily challenge streak, survival record and per-topic statistics
  • Your settings, such as language and the daily reminder
  • Whether you own "Remove ads" (a cached copy, re-checked with Google Play)

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Club data downloads

The app downloads updated club information and quiz questions from GitHub Pages (mathkn89.github.io). These requests contain no personal information from the app, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to GitHub, which hosts the files. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on browsing screens, an occasional full-screen ad between rounds, and an optional video you can choose to watch for a second chance in Survival mode. Ads are never shown while you are answering a question.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language) and app information
  • How you interact with ads (views and taps)

Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. You can accept or refuse personalised advertising, or choose individual purposes and partners. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised from your data.

You can change your choice at any time in the app under History → Settings → Privacy settings.

On any Android phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

The "Remove ads" purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details, name or email. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. It is created on your device; no notification service or server is involved, and no data is sent.

Children

The app is a general-audience football quiz and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app keeps your data only on your phone, you control it directly: change it in the app, or delete it by uninstalling. For data held by Google, use the links above or your Google Account. For anything else, email mathias@riebe.no.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
